01Meet Your Trainer

Warm, interactive and practical, grounded in psychology.

A graduate of the Bachelor of Psychology (Hons) from Universiti Pendidikan Sultan Idris with a CGPA of 3.89, I received the Royal Education Award (Pingat Jaya Cemerlang) in 2024 and the Outstanding Graduate Award from Persatuan Siswazah Wanita Malaysia in 2025.

My experience spans the corporate, education and government sectors: as a Learning & Development professional at UOA Academy, a Perdana Fellow in the Office of the Minister of Education, and a Malaysian delegate to the Ship for Southeast Asian and Japanese Youth Programme (SSEAYP).

My sessions are warm, interactive and practical, grounded in psychology and real-life experience.

02Training Topics

Sessions tailored to your audience, objectives and duration.

Port Healing: Stress and Emotional Awareness

Recognise the signs of stress, understand emotions and build simple self-care habits that protect mental wellbeing.

For: Youth, employees, community groupsFormat: Talk or interactive workshop

Bounce Back: Resilience and Motivation for Students

Turn setbacks such as disappointing results into a plan forward, rebuilding confidence, motivation and study direction.

For: Students, scholarship recipientsFormat: Workshop or motivational talk

Lead with Confidence: Youth Leadership and Public Speaking

Discover personal leadership style, speak with confidence and lead teams and programmes that create real impact.

For: University students, youth leadersFormat: Workshop or camp module

Mind Matters: Mental Health and Digital Wellbeing

Raise awareness of mental health and healthy technology use, including gadget dependency among children and teens.

For: Parents, teachers, children, residents’ associationsFormat: Talk or forum panel

Grow Forward: Personal Growth and Career Readiness

Build self-awareness, a growth mindset and the soft skills needed to move confidently from study into work.

For: Graduates, asnaf and career switchers, new hiresFormat: Workshop
