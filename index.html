01About

I help organisations develop their people, and I can stand in front of the room too.

I am a Learning & Growth Specialist at UOA Academy, part of UOA Group Berhad, reporting directly to the Chief Learning Officer. I manage learning programmes end to end: identifying needs, planning, selecting participants, coordinating delivery, engaging stakeholders and reporting on results.

My background in psychology shapes how I design learning. I pay attention to how adults learn, what motivates them, and whether what happens in a training room carries over to the job.

Alongside my role, I am an active trainer and facilitator. Agencies such as KBS, JBSN, Yayasan Tenaga Nasional and Lembaga Zakat Selangor have invited me to run sessions on wellbeing, motivation and career readiness. That experience keeps my programme design practical.
